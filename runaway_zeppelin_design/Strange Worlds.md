# Map
The world map is fully 3d, but is separated in hexagonal tiles. It is covered in Fog Of War that will be revealed within a certain radius around the zeppelin. Fog Of War that has been revealed but where the zeppelin has no active sight to will be greyed out.
The map has to be big enough that a player cannot survive on the initial resources they have been granted. 
POIs should be far enough away to force the player to make decisions, but there should still be a few POIs visible on every tile so that a decision can be made.
There should be obstacles (like mountain ranges), where a certain cruising height has to be reached in order to fly over them. If the zeppelin is damaged or does not have enough lift, a player has to circumvent these obstacles.
Pathfinding on the map is done automatically. Pathfinding will try to find the most cost-efficient way (via A*), but a player can chain waypoints to effectively override the automatic pathfinding.
Some POIs will allow you to add map portions to your map, gaining more insight about certain regions.
# Factions
There are NPCs that you can encounter and talk to/trade with. These NPCs are either people who have stranded in the strange worlds before or are survivors of previous runs.
Stranded people have built tribes, villages and cities and are fully interactable. They have been here years or decades and have reached different levels of civilization.
Survivors are from the previous run. Each new run, there is a chance that survivors die and leave stuff behind. Survivors want to be rescued while stranded people can only be recruited when the standing with their belonging faction is high enough. Sometimes, storylines will emerge where a person wants to join the crew/passengers.
