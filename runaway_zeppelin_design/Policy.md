In the Pursers cabin a player can enact policies to change the simulation behavior. Policies that can be enacted could be:
* Extra rations => improves morale in the crew by assigning them an extra ration per day
* Quarantine => confines Passengers to quarter in order to prevent sicknesses spreading
* Emergency rations => passengers only get rations too, decreasing their morale but keeping them fed
* Conserve water => people don't wash anymore and will drink less
* etc.

Policies can be enabled and disabled at any time. They can be one-off (like "hand out rum right now") or they can be standing (like quarantine). Enabling and disabling policies will take a little time until they are in effect - until then they can be canceled freely. Once they are in effect, they cost or save resources usually in favor of another one (hand out extra rations will cost more rations but improve morale).
Policies only affect live aboard the zeppelin and are about resource distribution.
After disabling a morale boosting policy, NPCs will receive a debuff on their morale for a short time.
