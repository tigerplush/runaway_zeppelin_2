There is two types of NPCs: [[#Crew Members]] and [[#Passengers]].
## Crew Members
Crew members are more resilient than passengers. They loose [[Morale]] slower and are accustomed to uncomfortable living. They only need a bunk bed and a sea sack. They also don't loose morale when living on rations.
Crew Members are assigned to shifts and the player can reassign them to different shifts.
Engineers need to be available round the clock. Cooks only need to be active during the day. A helmsman needs to be active whenever the player wants to move the zeppelin.
## Passengers
Passengers need a bed in a cabin and access to their luggage. They need three square meals a day and quickly loose morale when inconvenienced. Some passengers have day [[#Jobs]] that can be utilized in [[Event]]s.
# Needs
Each NPC has stats for their
* food => decreases every tick, is satiated by food
* drink => decreases every tick, is satiated by drink
* rest => decreases every tick, is satiated by sleep
* hygiene => decreases slowly, is satiated by washing (converting fresh water into grey water)
* socializing => decreases slowly, is satiated automatically when people want to socialize they will meet up and do so. 
* entertainment => sinks extremely slowly, is satiated by entertainment events (someone playing their instrument)
* luxury => Sinks extremely slowly, is satiated by giving out luxury goods (cigars, brandy, etc.)
* safety => how "safe" an NPC feels, is influenced by decisions and events
* health => how healthy an NPC is, NPCs can get sick when they are not fed, hydrated or cleaned properly, or as a result of an expedition. If an NPC gets sick, they get the "Sick" trait and their health will slowly tick down.
## NPC death

An NPC can die in a number of ways: From starvation or dehydration, by being thrown out of the zeppelin by the player. An NPC can also die during expeditions or from sicknesses/illnesses.
## Vision
Three "times" a day there will be food service where everyone is fed. The food service is different for crew and passengers. Crew will simply meet in the mess hall and eat their rations (staggered, because the mess hall only fits a few people at a time). For passenger service, the cook and his helpers go into the kitchen and prepare meals from raw foods which is then served to the passengers.
# Traits
All NPCs have a random number of traits. These can be good, bad or neutral.
* Night Owl => no penalty at night/more rest during day?
* Gluttonous => Needs two portions of food for each dinner
* etc.
# Jobs
Each crew member starts with a job. Crew members are generated in such a way, that all jobs on all shifts are filled with some redundancy (so when one helmsmen dies, there is still a second helmsman). Passengers might have jobs. Passengers with jobs are considered specialists. These specialists can give bonuses in [[Event]]s or greatly improve [[Event]] outcomes.
Possible Jobs could be: Engineer, Mechanic, Cartographer, Cook etc.
