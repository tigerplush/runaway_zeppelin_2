When a run was victoriously, the player gets a meta progression currency. With this currency, they can buy upgrades to the zeppelin for future runs. It is even possible to buy different zeppelin models.

Currency will be earned for every passenger that arrives back home. There is an additional menu where cargo can be sold for money. Some cargo the zeppelin stocks with has the explicit purpose of making money between runs (such as Mailbags). Sometimes the player finds artifacts during Expeditions that can be sold. Unsold cargo will be available to stock the zeppelin with for the next run.

On a loss, the player loses everything and will earn no meta progression currency - but lost zeppelins can be referenced in future runs: A wreck of the zeppelin with some of its loot still intact or an NPC from a previous run surviving on an island that you can pick up.

# Possible Upgrades 
Possible upgrades are different engines, different radios, starting with upgraded rooms or converted cargo space.
